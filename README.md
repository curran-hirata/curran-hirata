### FX hedging: EUR 3,000,000 receivable (online-degrees)

A five-stage project on how a U.S. online-degree provider should hedge a EUR 3,000,000 receivable due in 273 days. The recommendation: take a forward on a fresh quote at or above the covered-parity forward of 1.1118, use the money-market hedge if the quote is lower, and do not buy the put.
